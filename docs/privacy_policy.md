# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

ABG Analyzer Pro is an educational and clinical support application designed for arterial blood gas analysis.

## Data Collection

The application does not collect personal information such as:
- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- User identity information

No user account is required.

## Medical Data

Values entered by the user, including:
- pH
- PaCO2
- HCO3-
- Electrolytes

are processed locally on the device for calculation and interpretation.

No patient information or entered medical data is transmitted to external servers.

## Data Sharing

ABG Analyzer Pro does not share personal information or medical data with third parties.

## User Responsibility

Users should avoid entering identifiable patient information.

## Medical Disclaimer

This application is an educational and decision-support tool. It does not replace professional medical judgment, diagnosis, or patient evaluation.
