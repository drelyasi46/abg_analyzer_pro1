# Scientific References

ABG Analyzer Pro is designed based on established medical principles for arterial blood gas interpretation and acid-base disorders.

References:

1. Nelson Textbook of Pediatrics, 22nd Edition, 2024.
   Sections related to acid-base disorders, fluids and electrolytes.

2. Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 22nd Edition.
   Acid-base physiology and metabolic and respiratory disorders.

3. Marino's The ICU Book, 5th Edition.
   Interpretation of arterial blood gases and critical care acid-base disorders.

4. UpToDate (2026).
   - Approach to acid-base disorders.
   - Metabolic acidosis.
   - Respiratory acid-base disorders.

5. American Thoracic Society educational resources related to respiratory physiology and clinical interpretation.

The application is intended as an educational and clinical support tool and does not replace physician judgment or complete clinical evaluation.
